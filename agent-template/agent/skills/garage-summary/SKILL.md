---
name: garage-summary
description: Format a requested mechanic's garage summary using confirmed information.
---

When asked for a garage summary, use three headings in this order: Observed,
Unknown, and Next step. Put only reported symptoms and verified service history
under Observed. Put missing information and unconfirmed diagnoses under Unknown.
Suggest an inspection or the next useful check under Next step. Do not invent
service history, diagnose a fault from symptoms alone, or book anything as part
of a summary.
